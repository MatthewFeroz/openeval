01

About this policy

OpenEval is operated by Matthew Feroz. This policy covers the OpenEval website and hosted evaluation workspace at openeval.howaiworks.io. It explains what happens to information when you sign in, connect a worker, import results, or share an evaluation. Software you run independently and third-party model providers also have their own data practices.

02

Information we process

Account information includes your email address, account identifier, and profile information supplied by Clerk or your selected Google or GitHub sign-in provider. Social sign-in requests basic identity and email access; it does not grant OpenEval access to your private repositories or Google Drive.

Workspace information includes reports you import, evaluation settings, scores, costs, task and model identifiers, project edits, memberships, and any traces or artifacts you choose to upload. Such artifacts can contain prompts, source code, outputs, and personal information. Review them before uploading or sharing.

Connected workers send their connection status, capabilities, approved profiles, job progress, and results to the workspace. Provider keys configured through the connected-worker setup remain in that worker’s local state. The worker sends requests to the providers you configure. Local task files and raw logs remain in the execution environment unless a feature you use uploads them.

Our infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, browser details, request times, and operational logs to deliver and secure the service. We also receive information you send when requesting support.

03

How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts, operate workspaces and connected evaluations, display results, apply sharing permissions, investigate failures and abuse, and answer support requests. OpenEval does not use private evaluation content to train AI models. Model providers you select handle submitted content under their own terms and data controls.

04

Service providers and sharing

Clerk handles authentication. Google and GitHub handle their respective optional sign-in flows. Vercel hosts the website and may store generated export files. Convex stores hosted workspace records and runs backend functions. Cloudflare provides domain and networking services. When enabled, Vercel Web Analytics measures public-site usage and Sentry receives filtered application error diagnostics.

Your selected model providers and gateways, including Merge Gateway when configured, receive the content necessary to execute your requests. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

Reports become accessible to others when you grant access or enable a sharing feature. Recipients may retain downloaded copies. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to address abuse, protect users, or resolve a security incident. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

05

Cookies and local storage

Authentication uses cookies and related browser storage to keep you signed in and protect your session. Browser and worker storage may retain settings, credentials, drafts, caches, and local evaluation files. You can clear browser storage or remove local worker data, but doing so may end sessions or interrupt a connection. Clearing local data does not delete records already uploaded to the hosted workspace.

06

Retention and your choices

We keep hosted information for as long as needed to operate your workspace, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. Retention varies by record type and infrastructure provider; backups and security records may persist after removal from the active workspace.

Use available sharing and deletion controls to manage reports. Deleting a report does not necessarily remove evaluation history, separately combined reports, downloaded copies, or files held by your workers and model providers. Contact us to request account deletion or assistance with associated hosted data. We may verify that you control the account before acting.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information, or object to or restrict its processing. Send requests to the contact below. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

07

Security and international processing

We use account authentication, permission checks, and encrypted network connections to protect hosted information. No service can guarantee complete security. Keep account credentials and worker access secure, and avoid uploading secrets that are not needed for an evaluation. Infrastructure providers may process information in countries other than where you live, including the United States.

08

Children, changes, and contact

OpenEval is intended for adults and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Material changes affecting existing users will be communicated through the service or another appropriate channel.

For privacy questions, data requests, or security concerns, contact Matthew Feroz at matthewferoz@gmail.com.
